# Trace-Class Factorization and Spectral Trace for a Fixed Character

Fix a positive strip width and let $E$ be the radial space generated by polynomial zero modes. The strongly continuous translation group on $E$ is $U_t f(x)=f(x+t)$. The previous stage established that the generator resolvent $R_b=(bI-A)^{-1}$ is Hilbert–Schmidt under the zero-count hypothesis. The idelic quotient application takes the density conclusion of manuscript Theorem 10.2.2.1 as input. The factorization follows leecrossley's public repair proposal. [Hilbert–Schmidt estimate](RESOLVENT_HILBERT_SCHMIDT.md) · [Public factorization](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/issues/1169#issuecomment-7057208991)

## Kernel differentiation and the generator domain

For $k\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R,\mathbb C)$, define $T_k x=\int_{\mathbb R} k(t)U_t x\,dt$ by a vector-valued Bochner integral. The translation bound and compact support make $T_k$ bounded. The group law and the change of variables $t\mapsto t+h$ give

$$
U_h T_k x = \int_{\mathbb R} k(t-h)U_t x\,dt
$$

For $h$ in a neighborhood of zero, the shifted supports of $k$ and $k'$ lie in a fixed compact interval. On it, $k'$ and $\|U_t\|$ are uniformly bounded, giving an integrable majorant for differentiation. The orbit derivative is $-T_{k'}x$. Therefore $T_kx\in D(A)$ and $AT_kx=-T_{k'}x$. Applying the argument to $k'$ also gives $T_kx\in D(A^2)$.

Lean proves the change of variables from the group law and integrability and derives the orbit derivative under the differentiation and domination hypotheses. The majorant on the weighted space is constructed analytically above.

## Factorization through two resolvents

Applying $(bI-A)$ twice gives

$$
(bI-A)^2 T_k = T_{b^2k+2bk'+k''},\qquad
T_k=R_b^2 D_k,\quad D_k=T_{b^2k+2bk'+k''}.
$$

Lean proves the factorization vector by vector from the two orbit derivatives and the inverse identity on the generator domain. The kernel defining $D_k$ has compact support, so $D_k$ is bounded.

For an orthonormal basis $(e_j)$, $R_b^2D_k$ has the following rank-one expansion.

$$
x\longmapsto
\sum_j R_b e_j\,\langle D_k^*R_b^*e_j,x\rangle.
$$

Parseval applied to the rows and columns shows that the squared column norms of $R_b^*$ are summable. Cauchy–Schwarz and the bound on $\|D_k\|$ give summability of the rank-one operator norms. Lean verifies that summability and the exact sum in operator norm. Each rank-one term has trace norm equal to its operator norm. Completeness of $S_1$ gives convergence of the same partial sums in trace norm. The bound $\|S\|\le\|S\|_1$ identifies the two limits. This Schatten-space step uses standard operator theory.

## Diagonal sum and orthogonal compression

Enumerate the modes at each zero in degrees $0,\ldots,n_\chi(z)-1$, preserving that order within each zero. Every finite subset is independent by linear independence of exponential polynomials with distinct exponents. This enumeration gives a flag of finite-dimensional spaces invariant under $U_t$. The $j$th Gram–Schmidt vector is a multiple of the $j$th mode plus an element of the preceding space, so the diagonal coordinate of $U_t e_j$ is $e^{z_jt}$. Passing the coordinate functional through the integral gives

$$
\langle e_j,T_ke_j\rangle=M_k(z_j),\qquad
M_k(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}k(t)e^{zt}\,dt.
$$

Lean verifies the complex diagonal value and $\sum_j|M_k(z_j)|<\infty$ under the $R_b^2D_k$ factorization. The orthonormal-basis trace theorem for trace-class operators then gives the formula below for retained multiplicities $n_\chi(z)$.

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{E_\chi}(T_k)=\sum_z n_\chi(z) M_k(z).
$$

Let $P_N$ be the orthogonal projection onto the first $N$ basis vectors. Write each term of the rank-one expansion as $a_j\otimes b_j^*$. Its compression is $P_N(a_j\otimes b_j^*)P_N=(P_Na_j)\otimes(P_Nb_j)^*$. Strong convergence $P_N\to I$ makes each trace norm error tend to zero. The error is bounded by $2\|a_j\|\|b_j\|$. Dominated convergence for the sum gives $\|P_N T_kP_N-T_k\|_1\to0$. This is an analytic trace norm argument.

The result applies to the specified fixed-character radial model. The idelic quotient application requires the manuscript's density input. The all-character sum is treated in the follow-up. The manuscript's biorthogonal partial sums and geometric truncation require additional arguments. [Trace over all character components](GLOBAL_CHARACTER_TRACE.md)
